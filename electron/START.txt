Aion 2 Progress

Run Aion2Progress.exe from this folder. Leave the other files here. The app needs them.

Your roster is saved as aion2-tracker.json in this same folder. "Keep this window above the game" is saved as aion2-window.json.

The app reads public character pages. Odyle, Qina, and the other wallet numbers are typed in, then they count forward on their clocks. It does not attach to the game.
